The Last Weather Beacon

A beacon on the Nacre approach has started broadcasting after six years of silence. Its old coordinates put the signal inside a restricted landing corridor.

The survey pilot at the flight desk offers to guide the crew through the corridor in exchange for a copy of the beacon log.
