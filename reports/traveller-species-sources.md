# Traveller 2E species source checklist

The 24 November 2026 rescan checked filenames and PDF title metadata for 2,080 PDFs and partial PDFs, plus member names in 35 archives. The Mongoose 2E folder contained 95 PDF paths. The complete books listed below did not appear in those results. The Journal files with similar names are older editions or collections.

### Remaining evidence gaps

Otarri, Chirpers, and Kursae have local 2E references. The Otarri entry identifies their habitat, the Chirper section describes their relationship to Droyne, and the Kursae entry concerns an archaeological record. Further 2E anatomy and applicable encounter or character rules would support additional options.

The local *Aliens of Charted Space Vol. 1* copies are the earlier 2E printing. The [2026 update](https://www.mongoosepublishing.com/products/aliens-of-charted-space-vol-1-update-2026) would help check the existing Aslan, K’kree, Vargr, and Zhodani profiles against the revised book.
