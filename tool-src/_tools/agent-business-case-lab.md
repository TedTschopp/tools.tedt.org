## What it does

Use the lab to compare a paid service or lower operating costs against a baseline. In the operating cost case, enter separate results for a candidate upgrade with and without a workaround. Change whether you can cancel the support contract to see what actually leaves the budget.

## How to use it

1. Choose **Growth** or **Operating cost** and open the distributor example, or start blank.
2. Enter a common monthly volume and the quality, turnaround, and financial targets.
3. Select each configuration and enter its costs and results. Mark whether the figures are illustrative, estimated, or measured.
4. Compare the year-one economics and check which options meet your targets.

The examples are fictional. The lab calculates in your browser using the numbers you enter and projects a steady month over a year. See the [companion article](https://tedt.org/The-Two-Business-Cases-for-AI-Agents/) for the worked example.
